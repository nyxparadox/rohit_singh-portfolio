THE EXPERIENCE

Learning how industry actually works.

The training focused on understanding how instrumentation, control systems and industrial communication work together inside a continuous-process manufacturing plant.

Rather than studying individual components in isolation, the training provided exposure to the relationship between field devices, controllers, communication networks and supervisory systems.

INDUSTRIAL ENVIRONMENT

Inside a cement manufacturing plant.

The plant environment connects raw-material handling, process control, instrumentation, automation and final product handling into one continuous industrial system.

TRAINING PROGRESSION

Five weeks. One expanding system view.

01
24 — 30 JUN

Plant Orientation & Field Instruments

Plant induction, safety training, cement-manufacturing overview and study of RTDs, thermocouples, proximity, limit, pull-cord, belt-sway and zero-speed switches.

02
01 — 07 JUL

Pressure, Actuation & DCS Introduction

Pressure transmitters, load cells, HYDAC EDS switches, solenoid systems and introduction to ABB 800xA / AC 800M.

03
08 — 14 JUL

DCS Architecture & Control Systems

Continued study of DCS architecture, CI854 Profibus modules and the relationship between PLC and DCS control architectures.

04
15 — 21 JUL

Kiln & Material Handling Systems

Exposure to kiln systems, material handling and automation associated with major plant process equipment.

05
22 — 27 JUL

Drives, Gas Analysis & Technical Documentation

Exposure to power-electronics applications, gas-analysis systems, industrial observations and consolidation of the training report.
